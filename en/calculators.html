Calculators

Eight calculators covering severance and notice pay, overtime, annual leave and unemployment benefit. All run on the current 2026 ceiling, minimum wage and SGK parameters, and each shows the statutory provision it rests on.

Severance Pay

30 days of dressed gross wage per full year of service, including the 2026 ceiling and stamp duty.

1475 s.K. m.14 Calculate →

Notice Pay

Notice period of 2–8 weeks based on service, and the corresponding pay.

Overtime Pay

50% premium for work beyond 45 hours a week, with the 270-hour cap check.

Annual Leave Pay

Graduated leave entitlement (14/20/26 days) and pay for unused leave.

Weekly Rest Day Pay

An additional 1.5 daily wages for each rest day worked.
