Loss of Earning Capacity Calculator

Loss of Earning Capacity Calculator

Get a rough estimate of pecuniary damages for permanent incapacity following an occupational accident or disease.

Basis: Code of Obligations, Arts. 54–55 and Law No. 5510, Art. 13 · Active period to age 60; passive period over remaining life expectancy at the minimum wage

IMPORTANT: This is a rough estimate only. The actual calculation is made by a court-appointed actuary using the TRH-2010 mortality table, the progressive annuity method, a 10% equitable reduction and the set-off of SGK benefits. Non-pecuniary damages are claimed separately.
