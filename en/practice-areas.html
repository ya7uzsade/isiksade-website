Our practice areas.

01 Practice Area

Labour & Social Security Law

Under Labour Act No. 4857 and social security legislation, we advise and litigate for both employees and employers across the entire employment relationship. Mandatory mediation is managed diligently in light of current Court of Cassation precedents. Our firm has extensive experience in recovering the wage, severance and overtime claims of workers employed on construction projects both in Türkiye and abroad.

  • Severance, notice pay and overtime claims
  • Reinstatement lawsuits
  • Occupational accident & disease compensation
  • Service determination & social security disputes
  • Claims of construction workers in Türkiye and abroad
  • Drafting and termination of employment contracts
02 Practice Area

Real Estate Law

From drafting lease agreements to the legal management of construction projects; from zoning, expropriation and land registry legislation to urban transformation under Law No. 6306 — we advise and litigate at every stage of real estate matters.

  • Lease agreements and eviction lawsuits
  • Deed annulment and registration lawsuits
  • Expropriation and compensation assessment
  • Zoning legislation disputes
  • Urban transformation (Law 6306) advisory
  • Partition lawsuits
03 Practice Area

Enforcement & Bankruptcy Law

For creditors and debtors alike, we provide comprehensive services in initiating and managing enforcement proceedings, protecting debtor rights, managing bankruptcy and concordat processes, and litigating enforcement-related disputes.

  • Enforcement proceedings with and without judgment
  • Negative declaratory and restitution actions
  • Bankruptcy proceedings and lawsuits
  • Concordat process management
  • Finance, credit and leasing disputes
  • Criminal cases arising from enforcement law
04 Practice Area

Corporate Law

From incorporation to daily operations, from mergers and acquisitions to shareholder relations, we advise at every stage of commercial life with a preventive-law approach.

  • Incorporation and articles of association
  • Mergers and acquisitions
  • Shareholders' agreements
  • General assembly and board processes
  • Commercial contracts
  • Ongoing legal advisory
05 Practice Area

Contract Law

We stand by our clients in drafting, reviewing and negotiating all types of commercial and personal contracts, and in resolving contract disputes through settlement, mediation or litigation.

  • Contract drafting and review
  • Management of negotiation processes
  • Termination and compensation disputes
  • Confidentiality and non-compete agreements
  • Distribution and franchise agreements
  • Litigation of contractual disputes
06 Practice Area

Family & Inheritance Law

With the sensitivity family law requires and confidentiality as a priority, we assist in divorce, alimony and custody processes, liquidation of matrimonial property, preparation of wills and division of estates.

  • Contested and uncontested divorce
  • Alimony and custody cases
  • Liquidation of matrimonial property
  • Preparation of wills
  • Abatement and fraudulent transfer claims
  • Certificates of inheritance and transfers
07 Practice Area

Criminal Law

We provide defence counsel for suspects and defendants as well as victim representation during investigation and prosecution; we are particularly experienced in negligence cases arising from occupational accidents.

  • Defence at the investigation stage
  • Felony cases
  • Criminal cases arising from occupational accidents
  • Victim representation
  • Appeals to regional courts and Court of Cassation
  • Reconciliation processes
