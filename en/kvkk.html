1. Personal Data Processed

Through our website and contact forms, we process your name and surname, telephone number, e-mail address and any personal data contained in the message you send us. Where an attorney-client relationship is established, other data required by the nature of the engagement may also be processed.

2. Purposes and Legal Grounds

Your personal data are processed for the purposes of responding to consultation requests, providing legal services, carrying out communications and fulfilling legal obligations, on the legal grounds set out in Article 5/2 of the KVKK: establishment and performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations and legitimate interest.

3. Transfer of Data

Your personal data are not shared with third parties without your explicit consent, except with authorised public institutions and judicial authorities where legally required. The attorney's duty of professional secrecy governs all processes.

4. Method of Collection and Retention

Your data are collected electronically via website forms, e-mail and telephone. Data are retained for as long as required by the processing purposes and, in any event, for the limitation periods prescribed by applicable legislation, after which they are deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

5. Your Rights under Article 11 of the KVKK

  • To learn whether your personal data are processed and to request information in this regard,
  • To learn the purpose of processing and whether data are used in accordance with that purpose,
  • To request rectification of incomplete or inaccurate data,
  • To request erasure or destruction of data within the framework of Article 7 of the KVKK,
  • To object to a result arising against you from analysis exclusively by automated systems,
  • To claim compensation for damage arising from unlawful processing.

6. Applications

You may submit requests concerning your rights, together with documents verifying your identity, to info@isiksade.com or in writing to our postal address above. Applications are concluded within thirty days at the latest pursuant to Article 13 of the KVKK.
